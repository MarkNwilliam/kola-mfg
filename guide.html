1. Getting started

  1. Open Kola and set your sign-in. Choose the owner account and create a PIN (4–8 digits) or password — stored only on this device.
  2. Set up your business in Business settings: name, currency (UGX by default), logo, and the header/footer printed on reports.
  3. Add your products and units (bag, sachet, crate…).
  4. Add your team in Users & Roles and set each person's PIN.
  5. Start recording in Sales, Stock and Planning. Your Today dashboard fills automatically.
Tip: press Lock in the top-right when you step away. Kola also locks itself after inactivity.

2. Daily basics

Today is your dashboard: daily sales, stock value, expiring batches, receivables and low stock. Open it first thing every morning.

  • Record a sale: Sales → new order → pick products and quantity. Stock reduces automatically (FEFO).
  • Take payment: record the payment against the customer in Sales/Debtors.
  • Receive stock: Stock & Expiry → add a batch with quantity, cost and expiry date.

3. Sales, customers & credit

  • Sales & Orders — record sales and invoices.
  • Distributors — customers/wholesalers with credit limits and terms.
  • Receivables — who owes you, ageing (0–30/31–60/61–90/90+) and overdue amounts.
  • Contracts — agreements and their expiry dates.
  • Distributor Map — map distributors by location (needs internet for map tiles).

4. Stock, expiry & stores

Kola tracks every batch and always tells you what to sell first (FEFO — first expired, first out).

  • Stock & Expiry — batches, quantities, values and expiry alerts.
  • Batch Report and Supply Plan — what to reorder and when.
  • Stores & transfers — move stock between locations.
  • Units — define pack sizes and conversions once, reuse everywhere.

5. Manufacturing & production

  • Planning — schedule runs, assign steps and people, track status.
  • Formulations — recipes/BOMs with quantities per batch.
  • Excellence & OEE — availability, performance, quality and end-of-day output.
  • Simulation — model “can we hit this deadline with this many people?”.
  • Run Checks — capture line checks and templates during production.

6. Quality & food safety

  • Quality Lab — log QC tests with results and limits.
  • Specifications — product specs and parameters (with computed formulas).
  • Inspections — planned and ad-hoc inspections with outcomes.
  • HACCP Plan — build and print your HACCP plan and steps.
  • FMECA — failure modes, risk scores and mitigations.
  • Compliance — licences, certificates and their expiry.
  • Scan (OCR) — photograph a document and pull the text in (first use downloads a language model — needs internet once).

7. People & workforce

  • Workforce — staff records, shifts/roster, attendance and labour cost.
  • Manpower — plan labour against work.
  • Users & Roles — who can sign in and what they can do.

8. EHS & safety

EHS — record incidents, risks (with scores), waste and staff training.

9. Assets & documents

  • Assets — equipment, maintenance schedules, calibrations, breakdowns (downtime) and usage.
  • Documents — SOPs, certificates, policies, with expiry reminders.

10. Finance

  • Invoices & Docs — raise and print invoices, record expenses.
  • Ledger — double-entry accounts, trial balance, profit & loss, balance sheet.
  • Contracts — commercial agreements and renewals.

11. Insights & audit

  • Insights — trends, top products, dead stock, reorder suggestions and forecasting.
  • Audit — a full log of who did what and when.

12. Business settings

  • Business settings — business details, currency, logo, and the header/footer/disclaimer printed on every report.
  • Categories — your own product/document categories.
  • Units — pack sizes and conversions.
  • Automations — scheduled digests and reminders (e.g. nightly backup, morning alerts).

13. Users, roles & security

  • Add people in Users & Roles. Each gets a role: owner/admin, manager, supervisor, operator, viewer, or your own custom role.
  • Set a PIN or password per user. To switch user, use the top-right selector — it locks and asks for that person's PIN.
  • Permitted actions are enforced server-side and logged in the Audit trail.
  • Press Lock to lock immediately; the app also locks itself after idle time.
Forgot a PIN? Sign in as an admin, open Users & Roles and set a new one.

14. Plan & billing

  • Plan & Billing shows your current plan and what's included.
  • Free plan — core records (sales, stock, products) and settings; free forever, no card.
  • Upgrade and pay by mobile money (MTN/Airtel) or card from the app. If you're offline, reconnect to pay.
  • Sign in with Google (owner) to tie your plan to your email — reinstalling can't restart your trial.

15. Data, backups & updates

  • Automatic backups run daily (last 14 kept) under Data & Updates.
  • “Back up now” saves a copy anywhere (USB, another folder). “Restore from a file…” brings your data back and restarts Kola.
  • Kola checks for updates on startup; when ready, click Restart & install. Your data is never touched by updates.

16. Offline & internet

Kola works fully offline. You only need internet to: pay for a plan, sign in with Google, use the map, or the first OCR download. Pages that need internet are marked ☁ and show a friendly notice; everything else keeps working.

17. Troubleshooting & FAQ

I can't sign in. Check Caps Lock; ask an admin to reset your PIN in Users & Roles.

A page says “needs the internet”. Reconnect and try again — your data is safe meanwhile.

My numbers look wrong. Check the currency and decimals in Business settings.

Something is broken. Restart Kola; if it persists, make a backup and call support.
